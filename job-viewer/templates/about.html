About STARFALL

Advancing Astronomical Image Processing through Automation

The Mission

STARFALL is a specialized platform designed to streamline the complex workflow of astronomical image processing. By bridging the gap between raw data acquisition and scientific analysis, we provide astronomers with a powerful, automated toolset to process high-volume image data with precision and reproducibility.

Automated Pipelines

Integrated Prefect flows ensure that your image processing jobs are handled reliably, with proactive status tracking and error management.

Advanced Visualization

Built-in FITS viewing with pan/zoom, colormaps, and WCS-aware region overlays lets you inspect products directly in your browser.

Scalable Processing

Designed to handle large datasets, STARFALL enables users to submit multiple jobs and track progress through a unified dashboard.
